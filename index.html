About

Who Is Michael Serdan?

First and foremost, I’m a concerned father and a husband. I’m married and, like many of you, I have two school-aged kids attending public schools in Windsor. I want our schools to better reflect the needs of these kids and their families.

Having travelled a bit and having experienced our system as well as others, I’m concerned about my kids entering the outside world unprepared. That concern is why I’m running for school board trustee.

In Windsor, you'll find me at the rink — coaching minor hockey with WMHA/RMHA, refereeing OMHA and WRA, and umpiring for the WUA. Community isn't a slogan; it's where I spend my time.

Platform • 10 Points • Parent-Focused
Platform

What I Stand For

Ten practical priorities — not slogans. Focused on safety, literacy, parents, and accountability.

01

Parental Rights

Parents must be fully informed about curriculum, policies, and their child's education. Transparency and meaningful consultation are non-negotiable.

02

Safe Schools for All

Every child deserves to learn in a physically and emotionally safe environment. Safety is the foundation of education.

03

Zero Tolerance for Bullying

Clear, consistent enforcement against bullying and violence. No exceptions, no bureaucracy that protects aggressors.

04

Bullying Education

Proactive programs teaching students how to identify, prevent, and deal with bullying — building resilience and empathy.

05

CORE & STEM Focus

Return to fundamentals: literacy, numeracy, critical thinking. Reduce dependence on devices. Audiobooks, calculators & AI only after mastery.

06

Financial Accountability

Every tax dollar tracked. Transparent budgets, audits, and spending that directly benefits classrooms and students.

07

Whole Child Support

Protect and expand school lunches, special education resources, extracurriculars, and athletics — essential, not extras.

08

Future Pathways

Real guidance through counseling, mentoring, and business partnerships. Honest pathways in AI, skilled trades, university and college.

09

Province-Wide Coalition

Build a united coalition of school boards and parents to advocate effectively at Queen's Park for local control.

10

Business Community Solutions

Find strong short-term solutions now through business sponsorships and partnerships — not waiting for provincial action.

5
CORE & STEM — Literacy First

Reading, writing, math, critical thinking — mastered before screens. Audiobooks, calculators, and AI are tools, not replacements. We teach fundamentals, then teach tools wisely.

Bottom Line

Safe kids. Informed parents. Accountable spending. Real preparation for jobs and life.

Frequently Asked

What does parental rights mean to you?

Full transparency on curriculum, policies, and school decisions affecting your child. No surprises, no closed doors.

How will you address bullying?

Zero tolerance means consistent consequences, plus education for students and staff on identifying and stopping it early.

What about technology and AI?

Teach kids to read, write, and think first. Then introduce calculators, audiobooks, and AI as supports — not crutches.

How will you handle budgets?

Open books, clear reporting, and spending that puts classrooms first. Every dollar should help a student.
